Wood 1 League: You now command 2 Corsair Ships, unlock the ANCHOR emergency brake, and the central Maelstrom Vortex is now active (radius 380, lethal eye radius 65)!

 The Goal

Be the first pirate fleet to deliver 35 Gold to your Home Port, or have the most delivered Gold after 120 turns.

 Rules

The battle takes place in a 1600×900 sea sector with inertial kinematics, variable cargo mass, and rotational ocean currents:

  • Inertial Sailing (SAIL x y power): Applies thrust (0 ≤ power ≤ 100) toward (x, y). Heavier gold cargo increases ship mass and reduces acceleration:
    accel = power / (1.0 + 0.15 * gold)
  • Emergency Brake (ANCHOR): Immediately cuts current velocity by 75% (vx *= 0.25, vy *= 0.25) and halves the pull of the Maelstrom for that turn.
  • The Maelstrom Vortex: Within 380 units of the center (800, 450), a counter-clockwise swirl and inward suction vector are added to every ship's velocity each turn. Any ship ending its movement within 65 units of (800, 450) is shipwrecked: it loses all carried gold and respawns at its Home Port!
  • Kinematics & Water Drag: Each turn, after thrust and currents are added to (vx, vy), the ship moves to (x + vx, y + vy), and water drag multiplies (vx, vy) by 0.78.
  • Plunder & Delivery: Passing within 85 units of a Treasure Chest loads its Gold aboard. Entering within 130 units of your Home Port delivers all carried Gold to your score!

 Game Input & Output

Initialization Input
Line 1: width height leagueLevel (1600 900 and league level).
Line 2: myPortX myPortY oppPortX oppPortY maelstromX maelstromY.
Input for One Game Turn
  • Line 1: shipCount, total number of ships.
  • Next shipCount lines: shipId owner x y vx vy gold cooldown (owner is 0 for your ships, listed first, and 1 for opponent ships).
  • Next line: chestCount, number of active floating chests.
  • Next chestCount lines: chestId cx cy chestGold.
Output for One Game Turn
Output one line per owned ship:
  • SAIL x y power (0 ≤ power ≤ 100)
  • or ANCHOR
